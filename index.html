FAQ

Common questions

Is this free for candidates?

Yes. Candidates never pay anything. Our fees are paid by the hiring company.

What kinds of roles do you focus on?

Founding BizOps, Chief of Staff, forward deployed and deployment roles, implementation, GTM and growth, and software engineering at AI and venture-backed startups.

What stage are the companies?

Mostly seed through Series C startups backed by leading investors.

How long does a placement take?

Usually a matter of weeks. One Series B client sent an offer letter 24 days after our first submissions.

Do you have remote or international roles?

Some, but options are limited. Most of our companies prefer candidates in or willing to relocate to the San Francisco Bay Area or New York.
