About

A recruiter who tells you the truth about fit.

Technical Recruiters is a small firm built around one idea: a few strong, honest submissions beat a pile of resumes, for the company and for the candidate.

SK

Sam Kwak is the founder of Technical Recruiters LLC. He works directly with founders and hiring leaders at venture-backed AI startups, and personally talks to every candidate before they are introduced to a company.

His searches concentrate on the operator and technical-delivery seats that early companies struggle to fill: Founding BizOps, Chief of Staff, forward deployed engineers and deployment strategists, implementation and solutions, early GTM, and software engineering.

How we work

  • Short lists, not pipelines. Companies see a few people who fit, each with a plain read on strengths and risks.
  • A straight answer on fit. If a role is not worth a candidate's time, we say so. If a search is outside our focus, we say that too.
  • Speed through focus. One Series B client sent an offer letter 24 days after our first submissions.
  • Free for candidates, always. Fees are paid by the hiring company.

Where we work

Most searches are in San Francisco and New York, where the AI startups and the candidate pool are concentrated. We also work with teams in Seattle, Los Angeles and Austin.
